To add your profile picture:
1. Save your profile photo as "hero-image.jpg" in the same folder as index.html
2. Make sure the image is:
   - Square aspect ratio (e.g., 400x400px) for best results
   - Good quality (at least 200x200px)
   - JPG, PNG, or WebP format

Recommended photo guidelines:
- Professional headshot
- Good lighting
- Clear background (or one that contrasts well)
- Face should be clearly visible
- Formal or business casual attire
